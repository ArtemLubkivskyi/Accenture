FAQ

Do the goats actually mow the lawn?

Yes. They graze grass and weeds on-site, which helps reduce mowing and chemical use in a natural way.

What if I have pets or safety concerns?

You can add yard notes and pet/safety details in your account so the crew can plan fencing and containment.

Is GoatCam included?

Yes. Every Starter plan includes live GoatCam during the visit plus the post-visit lawn report.

Can I pause during bad weather or travel?

Yes. The service is designed around simple scheduling, so you can pause visits when needed.
